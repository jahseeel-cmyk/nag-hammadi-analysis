# Marsanes Operator Grammar v1 — FROZEN CANDIDATE

Date frozen for holdout testing: 2026-09-19

## Scope

This grammar is derived from Marsanes only. It deliberately separates:
- directly/mostly observed structure,
- restoration-sensitive structure,
- attested but unparameterized performance language,
- esoteric interpretation.

It is not a claim that a hidden cipher has been solved.

## Structural operators

### OP1 SERIES(V)
Definition: arrange a defined vowel inventory in conventional sequence.
Marsanes anchor: C1 seven-vowel sequence; C2 five-vowel subsequence.
Evidence: HIGH for C1; MODERATE for C2 because one initial token is uncertain.
Control risk: HIGH — conventional Greek pedagogical ordering can explain the order.
Esoteric status: BACKGROUND unless coupled to soul/cosmic function.

### OP2 REPEAT3(X)
Definition: repeat a single vowel identity three times as one grouped unit.
Marsanes anchor: C3 male-soul vowel block.
Evidence: HIGH at token level; A and U groups partially restored, other groups strongly visible.
Computational finding: multiplicity contributes 0 bits to identifying the base vowel inside C3, so REPEAT3 is not the vowel-identity channel.
Esoteric hypothesis: may encode configuration/state/ritual operation rather than identity.

### OP3 GROUP(sequence)
Definition: preserve explicit grouping boundaries among vowel or sound units.
Marsanes anchor: triplets, diphthong lists, repeated groups.
Evidence: HIGH that grouping exists; exact semantics unresolved.
Esoteric hypothesis: grouping may distinguish soul configuration or ritual performance.

### OP4 COMBINE(C,V)
Definition: combine consonantal and vocal elements into structured syllabic/name forms.
Marsanes anchor: the text states that vowels/consonants constitute nomenclature of gods and angels; BA/GA/DA/ZA/THA-style material.
Evidence: HIGH for the general combination concept; LOW-MODERATE for a complete 5x7 grid because restoration dependence is substantial.
Esoteric hypothesis: sacred-name construction may be operational rather than merely lexical.

### OP5 SUBSTITUTE_VOWEL(frame,V)
Definition: hold a consonantal frame constant while changing the vowel series.
Marsanes anchor: reconstructed consonant-vowel rows.
Evidence: MODERATE as a pattern, LOW for full diplomatic completeness.
Rule: may be used only in RESTORED sensitivity analyses until critical-edition verification improves the secure cell count.

### OP6 CONCATENATE(X,Y,...)
Definition: join simple vocalic/syllabic units into compounds such as diphthongs or longer strings.
Marsanes anchor: explicit simple-vowel vs two-sound/diphthong material.
Evidence: HIGH for existence of compound sound units; exact segmentation of damaged strings varies.
Esoteric hypothesis: compounds may be higher-order name/soul operations.

## Candidate transformational grammar

Minimal structural form:

BASE_SYMBOL
  -> GROUP
  -> {REPEAT3 | CONCATENATE | COMBINE}
  -> optional SUBSTITUTE_VOWEL within supported frame
  -> PERFORMANCE{PITCH, BEAT, SILENCE, IMPULSE} where textually licensed
  -> NAME / SOUL-CONFIGURATION / RITUAL-COSMOLOGICAL FUNCTION

This is a typed grammar, not a substitution cipher.

## Rejection rules

Downgrade the unified-grammar model if:
1. holdout evidence requires new operators not present here;
2. apparent success is explained by ordinary Greek school exercises;
3. success depends mainly on editorial restorations;
4. generic ritual-vowel texts match equally well;
5. operator boundaries cannot be defined without post-hoc segmentation.

No new operator may be added during Zostrianos v1 scoring. Any addition creates Grammar v2 and counts as a v1 holdout failure.
