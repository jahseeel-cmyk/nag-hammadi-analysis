# Investigations Department Priority — Three Steles of Seth

Status: TIER 1 CORE COMPARATIVE SOURCE
Effective date: 2026-09-19

## Why promoted

The Three Steles of Seth is no longer merely a consumed holdout. It produced two preregistered primary transduction chains and an explicit ritual reversal statement. Its holdout role is complete, so its data may now be used to refine the working model.

It is promoted to sit beside Marsanes as a core investigative source, with different jobs:

- MARSANES: grapheme/sound operations, soul configuration, sacred nomenclature, performance variables.
- THREE STELES: liturgical sequencing, blessing/silence/ascent, command/vision/salvation, ascent/descent reversal, sacred-language function.

## Core evidentiary rule

Three Steles data may refine the model, but it may not be retroactively used to claim that Marsanes itself contains a feature not independently present there.

## High-priority questions

1. Is the liturgical path structurally reversible or only narratively reversible?
2. Does sacred expression mediate both ascent and descent?
3. Is silence a transition state, terminal state, or gating operation?
4. Do name/voice/word/command occupy different functional layers?
5. Does the stele order correspond to ontological levels independently of the number three?
6. Is salvation/knowledge the output of articulation, perception, participation, or all three?
7. Can the full ritual sequence be encoded with a small directed grammar?

## Anti-overreach controls

- Modern editorial introduction is excluded from primary evidence coding.
- The title "Three Steles" and raw triadic recurrence are not treated as proof.
- The exact reversal of stele order is secure as ritual topology, but does not prove every theological emanation is reversible.
- Secondary scholarship must be audited separately from the primary text.
- Critical-edition/facsimile verification remains required for strongest philological claims.
