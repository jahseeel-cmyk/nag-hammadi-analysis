# Four-Way Esoteric Comparator Test v21 — PREREGISTRATION

Freeze date: 2026-09-21

## Purpose

Test whether the current Marsanes model is genuinely specific or merely captures broad esoteric motifs that recur in unrelated texts.

## Texts

A. Marsanes (ancient Sethian/Nag Hammadi target)
B. Historical Emerald Tablet / Tabula Smaragdina (medieval Arabic-Latin Hermetic text)
C. Maurice Doreal, Emerald Tablets of Thoth the Atlantean (20th-century occult comparator)
D. Sefer Yetzirah (late-antique/early-medieval Jewish letter-cosmology comparator; dating treated cautiously)

## Why Sefer Yetzirah is the added comparator

It is selected BEFORE scoring because it is structurally closer to Marsanes than the other two:
- technical letter classes;
- combinatory operations;
- cosmological correspondences;
- sacred language architecture.

This makes it a stronger adversarial comparator than a generic mystical text.

## Frozen feature set

F21-1 TECHNICAL_LETTER_CLASS
Explicit classification of letters/sounds into technical classes.

F21-2 COMBINATORIAL_GENERATION
Letters/sounds explicitly permuted, joined, grouped, or generated into formal sequences.

F21-3 PERFORMANCE_EXECUTION
Explicit instructions governing how sound is executed:
pitch, rhythm, silence, breath, timbre, repetition, attack/onset, directional delivery, etc.

F21-4 ACTIVE_SOUND_TRANSITION
Sound/voice is not merely symbolic but undergoes a specific active operation:
summon, activate, order, subject, transform, unify, produce, or equivalent.

F21-5 MANY_TO_ONE_SOUND
Plural sound-elements are explicitly coordinated/reduced into a singular sounded result.

F21-6 SACRED_NAMING
Technical letter/sound operations generate or regulate divine/angelic names or sacred nomenclature.

F21-7 METAPHYSICAL_BINDING
Letter/sound classes are explicitly mapped to soul, cosmos, divine powers, elements, planets, body, or ontological states.

F21-8 ASCENT_DESCENT
Explicit ascent/descent/return architecture.

F21-9 CORRESPONDENCE
An above/below, macrocosm/microcosm, same-pattern-across-levels principle.

F21-10 OPERATOR_SPECIFICITY
The text gives constrained local operators rather than broad metaphors such as "vibration," "light," or "oneness."

## Hypotheses

H21-A SPECIFICITY
Marsanes will score higher than Doreal and the historical Emerald Tablet on:
F21-1, F21-2, F21-3, F21-4, F21-10.

H21-B GENERIC_ESOTERIC_MOTIF CONTROL
Doreal will overlap strongly on:
F21-7, F21-8, F21-9,
but weakly on the technical phonological features.

H21-C HISTORICAL_EMERALD_TABLET CONTROL
The historical Emerald Tablet will overlap mainly on:
transformation, unity, ascent/descent, correspondence,
not on technical phonology/performance.

H21-D SEFER_YETZIRAH CHALLENGE
Sefer Yetzirah may equal or exceed Marsanes on:
F21-1, F21-2, F21-7,
but should remain lower on the specific Marsanes performance-semivowel mechanism F21-3/F21-4/F21-5.

If Sefer Yetzirah matches Marsanes on all technical/performance features, the Marsanes model is not unique.

## Scoring

Each feature per text:
2 = explicit and central
1 = present but secondary/ambiguous
0 = absent/not established

No partial points.

Primary outputs:
- raw feature matrix;
- technical specificity subtotal = F21-1+F21-2+F21-3+F21-4+F21-5+F21-6+F21-10
- generic esoteric subtotal = F21-7+F21-8+F21-9
- pairwise Jaccard similarity on binary presence (score >=1).

## Interpretation

Useful comparator:
a text that shares enough structure to challenge the model but differs on a discriminating layer.

Over-generic model warning:
Doreal scores nearly as highly as Marsanes on technical specificity.

Strong Marsanes-specificity result:
Doreal and historical Emerald Tablet share broad metaphysical motifs, while Sefer Yetzirah shares letter architecture, but Marsanes remains distinctive in active performance-governed phonetic transition.

## Anti-fitting

Do not:
- treat "vibration" as pitch/rhythm without explicit execution;
- treat "word" as technical letter grammar;
- treat mystical unity as many-to-one sound;
- use later Kabbalistic commentary as evidence for Sefer Yetzirah's base text;
- treat Doreal as ancient evidence;
- infer direct borrowing from similarity alone.
