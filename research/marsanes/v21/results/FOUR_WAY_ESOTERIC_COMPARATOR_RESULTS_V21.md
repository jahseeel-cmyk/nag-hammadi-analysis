## Historical control: Emerald Tablet

The historical Emerald Tablet is a short medieval Hermetic-alchemical text.

The earliest securely attested recension is Arabic; scholarship traces the Latin tradition back through Arabic witnesses, while a Greek original remains unproven.

Its core structure includes:
- above/below correspondence;
- all things from one;
- separation of subtle/gross;
- ascent from earth to heaven;
- descent back to earth;
- receipt of superior/inferior powers.

It does NOT provide:
- technical letter classes;
- semivowel taxonomy;
- letter combinatorics;
- pitch/rhythm/silence execution instructions;
- active sound transition.

Therefore it is useful mainly as a control for GENERIC TRANSDUCTION / ASCENT-DESCENT / CORRESPONDENCE language.

## Doreal comparator

Doreal's twentieth-century Emerald Tablets contain strong sound/performance motifs:
- chants that call down light;
- "high vibration";
- named words used for passage;
- silence practices;
- repeated vibration;
- sound/music/voice linked to liberation, unity, and ascent.

This creates a substantial surface resemblance to parts of our Marsanes model.

However, Doreal lacks the technical Greek phonological architecture that currently defines the strongest Marsanes result:
- no vowel/semivowel/mute technical taxonomy;
- no comparable consonant-vowel grammar;
- no semivowel activation mechanism;
- no explicit many-to-one phonetic operation of the Marsanes type.

Thus Doreal is a GOOD ADVERSARIAL CONTROL:
it shows that chant, vibration, ascent, unity, and sacred words are generic enough to recur in modern occult writing.

Any model that treats those broad motifs alone as proof would overfit.

## Sefer Yetzirah — the most useful added text

Sefer Yetzirah proved far more useful than the two Emerald comparators for the technical side.

The base text explicitly contains:
- twenty-two letters as formative principles;
- three mothers, seven doubles, twelve simples;
- letters carved/hewn/weighed/exchanged/combined;
- a 231-gate combinatorial architecture;
- letters formed through voice/breath and articulated in mouth positions;
- cosmic/body correspondences.

This makes it a serious structural challenge to Marsanes.

It demonstrates that:

LETTER TAXONOMY
+
COMBINATORIAL GENERATION
+
COSMIC CORRESPONDENCE

is NOT unique to Marsanes.

That is an important model correction.

## Dating caution for Sefer Yetzirah

Its date and redaction history are disputed.

Recent scholarship by Tzahi Weiss argues for a seventh-century Syrian context and emphasizes Syriac grammatical/theological parallels.
Other scholarship preserves broader late-antique/early-medieval ranges.

Therefore Sefer Yetzirah is used here as a COMPARATOR, not as evidence for direct influence on Marsanes.
