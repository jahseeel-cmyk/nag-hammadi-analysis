# v13 Pilot Dataset Notes

Rows: 59

This pilot exists only to debug the preregistered pipeline.

It deliberately includes:
- Marsanes B1-B4;
- already-audited Nag Hammadi/related rows;
- 13 Hekhalot gate-specific tokens;
- explicit PGM performance procedures from IV, V, and VII;
- Dionysius as a non-ritual prosodic control.

Important:
- This is NOT the final n>=120 dataset.
- The PGM expansion sampling rule is not yet complete.
- Some rows migrate prior project coding rather than being re-edited from critical editions in this pass.
- Hekhalot and Jeuian material are translation-level.
- The pilot should not be used to declare H13-1 through H13-4 supported/falsified.
- Its purpose is to detect leakage, label imbalance, unstable features, and coding gaps.

Known schema gap exposed during coding:
PGM V contains articulation/timbre instructions such as "open mouth, undulating like a wave" that are not fully represented by the frozen feature set.
They remain in notes rather than creating a post-hoc primary feature.
A future secondary exploratory feature may encode MOUTH_SHAPE/TIMBRE, but it cannot enter the preregistered primary v13 test.
