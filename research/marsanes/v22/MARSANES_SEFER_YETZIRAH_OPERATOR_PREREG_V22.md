# Marsanes vs Sefer Yetzirah Operator Comparison v22 — PREREGISTRATION

Freeze date: 2026-09-21

## Purpose

Test whether the structural resemblance between Marsanes and Sefer Yetzirah survives at the level of constrained OPERATORS rather than broad "letters create reality" motifs.

## Texts

A. Marsanes NHC X,1, especially the alphabetic/phonological block X25–32.
B. Sefer Yetzirah base text, with recension differences recorded rather than harmonized.

## Core question

Do the two texts instantiate the same operator chain:

CLASSIFY
-> COMBINE
-> VOICE/BREATH
-> FORM SOUL/COSMOS

or do they diverge at the transition mechanics?

## Frozen operator set

O22-1 CLASSIFY
Explicit partition of letters/sounds into technical classes.

O22-2 COMBINE
Explicit joining/permutation/exchange of letters/sounds into formal structures.

O22-3 VOICE_BREATH
Explicit association of the letters/sounds with voice, breath, mouth, or articulated speech.

O22-4 ACTIVE_TRANSITION
A verbal operator actively transforms or activates the sound/letter class.

O22-5 PERFORMANCE_GATE
Execution parameters beyond ordinary articulation:
pitch, rhythm/beat, silence, attack/onset, timbre, counted recitation, directional delivery, etc.

O22-6 MANY_TO_ONE
Multiple phonetic/letter elements are brought into one sounded result.

O22-7 SACRED_NAMING
Letter/sound operations generate or regulate divine/angelic names or sacred nomenclature.

O22-8 COSMIC_FORMATION
Letter operations explicitly form cosmos/elements/creation.

O22-9 SOUL_BODY_FORMATION
Letter operations explicitly map to or form soul/body/human structure.

O22-10 CORRESPONDENCE
Same letter architecture maps across multiple domains (cosmos/body/time/etc.).

## Competing models

### M22-A SHARED SACRED-LETTER TECHNOLOGY
Both texts share:
CLASSIFY + COMBINE + VOICE/BREATH + COSMIC/SOUL BINDING.

Prediction:
at least 6 of 10 operators overlap.

### M22-B MARSANES-SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE PHONOLOGY
Marsanes alone shows:
ACTIVE_TRANSITION + PERFORMANCE_GATE + MANY_TO_ONE
in a tightly local phonetic sequence.

Prediction:
Marsanes exceeds Sefer Yetzirah on O22-4/O22-5/O22-6.

### M22-C SEFER-YETZIRAH GENERATIVE SUPERIORITY
Sefer Yetzirah exceeds Marsanes in explicit combinatorial generation and domain-spanning correspondences.

Prediction:
Sefer Yetzirah equals/exceeds Marsanes on O22-2/O22-8/O22-9/O22-10.

### M22-D SUPERFICIAL ANALOGY
The texts share fewer than 6 operators, indicating that the resemblance is broad/esoteric rather than operational.

## Scoring

Each operator per text:
2 = explicit and central
1 = present but secondary/ambiguous
0 = absent/not established

No partial points.

Outputs:
- operator matrix;
- overlap count (presence = score >=1);
- weighted cosine similarity;
- operator-by-operator commentary;
- list of NON-OVERLAP operators.

## Evidence rules

Marsanes:
use project Coptic/translation and prior critical-edition audits.

Sefer Yetzirah:
use base-text translations/critical scholarship.
Where short/long recensions differ, code:
- CORE if present in both;
- RECENSION-SENSITIVE if only one.

Do not import later Kabbalistic commentary into base-text scoring.

## Anti-fitting

Do not:
- treat "breath" as ritual performance unless execution is prescribed;
- treat every cosmological correspondence as letter-generated unless text explicitly binds it to letters;
- treat divine names as sacred-naming if no letter operation produces/regulates them;
- assume historical contact from structural similarity;
- use later Zoharic or medieval Kabbalistic interpretations to upgrade Sefer Yetzirah.

## Decision rule

If M22-A and M22-B both pass:
conclude there is a broad shared sacred-letter technology, but Marsanes has a distinctive performance-phonology layer.

If M22-A passes and M22-B fails:
the systems may be more structurally convergent than currently thought.

If M22-A fails:
downgrade Sefer Yetzirah to a broad comparator only.

If M22-C passes:
treat Sefer Yetzirah as the better comparator for generative letter cosmology.
