## Unit of analysis

A TEXTUAL SEGMENT is the smallest continuous prose unit that expresses one dominant operation.

Segment boundaries are determined by:
- explicit topic shift;
- new grammatical/phonological class;
- explicit transition marker;
- new verbal operation;
- clear lacuna boundary.

Do not split a single clause merely to improve the hypothesis.

## Frozen segment labels

TAXONOMY
Definition/classification of vowels, consonants, semivowels, diphthongs, letter classes, or nomenclature without explicit generation/performance.

COMBINATION
Explicit joining, prefixing, suffixing, substitution, syllabification, or ordering of letter/sound classes.

SOUNDED_REALIZATION
Explicit production, summoning, voicing, sounding, or unification into sound.

PERFORMANCE
Explicit beat/ictus, pitch/tonal sharpness, silence, attack/onset/horme, breath, or other named execution parameter.

METAPHYSICAL_BINDING
Explicit binding of phonological/grammatical structures to soul shape, gods, angels, hidden powers, or nomenclature.

## Primary prediction P17-1

Every secure PERFORMANCE occurrence in X25–32 will occur in a segment that is either:
- SOUNDED_REALIZATION,
or
- immediately adjacent to and syntactically governing a SOUNDED_REALIZATION segment.

Success:

Failure:
any secure PERFORMANCE occurrence occurs in a purely TAXONOMY segment with no local sounded/combinatory function.
