# Frontier Research Charter — Marsanes Project

## Purpose

This project uses mainstream scholarship as a **reference baseline**, not as a final authority. Critical editions, philology, historical linguistics, and peer-reviewed scholarship establish what is currently defensible about the manuscript and its context. They do not set a ceiling on what may be investigated.

The project is explicitly allowed to pursue unconventional, esoteric, comparative, and intuition-generated hypotheses. The standard is not whether a hypothesis is conventional. The standard is whether it can be stated clearly, tested against the text, compared with alternatives, and either strengthened or weakened by evidence.

## Core Principle

**Open imagination + hard falsification.**

- Do not reject a hypothesis because it is unconventional.
- Do not accept a hypothesis because it feels meaningful.
- Treat intuition as a hypothesis generator.
- Convert intuition into predictions before checking the target evidence.
- Preserve negative results.
- Keep manuscript evidence, editorial restoration, published interpretation, and new hypothesis in separate layers.

## Dual-Track Method

### Track A — Baseline Reconstruction

Purpose: establish the strongest conventional account.

Sources:
- critical editions and facsimiles
- Coptic/Greek philology
- ancient grammar
- historical phonology
- peer-reviewed Marsanes/Sethian/Hermetic scholarship

Outputs:
- DIPLOMATIC corpus
- RESTORED corpus
- school/grammatical substrate
- historically defensible pronunciation models
- known scholarly interpretations

### Track B — Frontier Exploration

Purpose: ask questions not exhausted by the existing literature.

Candidate domains:
- internal transformation rules
- cross-text Sethian invariants
- Hermetic vocal structures
- theurgy and sacred-language practice
- Platonic and later-Platonic formal structure
- information theory and formal grammar
- number only where independently anchored by the text
- ritual performance variables only where textually or historically constrained

Track B may disagree with Track A, but it must state exactly where and why.

## Intuition-to-Test Protocol

For every intuition:

1. **Capture** — write it before searching for confirming examples.
2. **Translate** — turn it into one or more precise claims.
3. **Predict** — state what unseen pattern should appear if the claim is right.
4. **Falsify** — state what result would count against it.
5. **Control** — identify a mundane/null explanation.
6. **Freeze** — timestamp the prediction before holdout inspection.
7. **Test** — compare against diplomatic text first, then restored text.
8. **Audit** — record success, failure, ambiguity, or dependence on restoration.

Do not retroactively rewrite a failed prediction into a success.

## Connection Standard

A proposed new connection is not considered strong merely because two systems resemble one another.

A frontier connection should include:

- a textual anchor in Marsanes
- an independent comparator
- a plausible historical or structural bridge
- a specific shared mechanism, not only a symbolic resemblance
- a novel prediction
- at least one matched null/control
- an explicit falsifier

## Frontier Evidence States

- **OBSERVED** — visible manuscript or directly recoverable fact.
- **RESTORED** — editorial reconstruction.
- **ESTABLISHED** — strong published scholarly interpretation.
- **TESTABLE HYPOTHESIS** — clear prediction and falsifier.
- **SURVIVES CONTROLS** — beats predefined nulls/sensitivity tests.
- **PREDICTIVE** — succeeds on frozen holdout material.
- **SPECULATIVE** — interesting but not yet constrained.
- **FAILED** — contradicted by the predefined test.

## Anti-Pattern-Hunting Rules

Before interpreting a pattern, control for:
- sequence length
- symbol frequencies
- run lengths
- segmentation choices
- editorial restoration
- ordinary school exercises
- generic seven-vowel paradigms
- common late-antique ritual-vowel practices

A pattern that vanishes after these controls is not evidence of a unique Marsanes mechanism.

## Frontier Metrics

Use exact or reproducible measures where possible:

- collision counts
- H(T|R)
- I(T;R)
- normalized mutual information
- edit/transform cost
- minimum description length (MDL)
- exact null tail probability
- diplomatic-only signal retention
- holdout prediction with zero new rules

For a null-tail probability p, use information surprise only as a descriptive quantity:

S = -log2(p)

This is not a mystical number. It measures how unexpected a result is under a stated null model.

## Holdout Discipline

Model-fitting order:
1. Marsanes only
2. freeze operators
3. Zostrianos
4. Holy Book of the Great Invisible Spirit
5. Trimorphic Protennoia
6. Discourse on the Eighth and Ninth
7. only afterward broader Chaldean/theurgic/esoteric comparanda

If a holdout requires a new rule, record that as a failure of the frozen model before creating a revised model.

## Research Aim

The goal is not to force Marsanes into a preconceived occult system. The goal is to determine whether the text preserves a formal structure that existing scholarship has only partially characterized, and whether that structure yields reproducible, predictive relationships across independent material.

The project is permitted to go farther than current scholarship — but every step farther must become more explicit, not less testable.
