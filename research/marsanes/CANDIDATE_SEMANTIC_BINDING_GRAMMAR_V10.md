## Why the correction matters

The Greek loan verb hypotassein can be parsed as either:
- submit
or
- subject

in the Coptic syntax.

Therefore B4 could describe:
- consonants being subordinated to hidden gods,
- hidden gods being subjected/ordered through altered consonantal performance,
- or another ordering relation.

The project must not choose among these until the syntax and lexical evidence are resolved.
