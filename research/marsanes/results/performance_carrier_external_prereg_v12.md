# Performance-Carrier External Test v12 — SEALED PREREGISTRATION

Freeze date: 2026-09-20

## Question

Is sacred-token identity/function specified only by written lexical form, or can execution parameters form part of the ritual token?

Candidate model under test:
PERFORMANCE-CARRIER GRAMMAR v11

TOKEN = WRITTEN FORM + EXECUTION PARAMETERS

## Primary corpora

1. Greek Magical Papyri (procedural ritual evidence)
2. Iamblichus, De Mysteriis (theoretical/theurgical evidence)

## Control

Ordinary Greek grammatical/prosodic material:
pitch, quantity, accent, rhythm, and recitation may be discussed, but without explicit binding to divine/ritual efficacy.

## Frozen success criteria

PC001 — PROCEDURAL PERFORMANCE BINDING
At least three PGM procedures must explicitly pair a sacred utterance/name/formula with a non-lexical execution parameter such as:
- vocal quality,
- pitch/register,
- prolongation,
- breath control,
- silence/rest,
- onset/attack,
- rhythmic or repeated delivery.

Success requires the execution instruction to be part of the ritual procedure, not merely modern editorial description.

PC002 — EXECUTION-SENSITIVE EFFECT
At least one case must show that altering or specifying HOW a formula is voiced is textually tied to a ritual stage, recognition event, manifestation, passage, or effect.

Strongest form:
same or near-identical lexical material under different execution yields a different ritual function.

Weaker but acceptable:
specific execution is required for the effect and cannot be dropped without changing the prescribed rite.

PC003 — THEURGIC NON-INTERCHANGEABILITY
Iamblichus must explicitly argue that divine names/sounds/symbolic utterances cannot be freely translated or replaced because their traditional sound/form participates in ritual efficacy or divine connection.

PC004 — CONTROL DISCRIMINATION
Ordinary prosodic/grammatical sources may discuss pitch, accent, duration, rhythm, or silence, but do not bind these features to divine manifestation, ascent, protection, or other ritual effect in the same way.

PC005 — ANTI-ANACHRONISM
No claim may introduce:
- 432 Hz,
- planetary frequencies,
- modern musical note assignments,
- exact BPM,
- chakra frequencies,
unless explicitly present in the ancient source.

## Decision

STRONG_SUPPORT:
PC001, PC003, PC004, PC005 supported, and PC002 at least WEAKLY_SUPPORTED.

PARTIAL_SUPPORT:
PC001 and PC003 supported, but PC002 not established.

WEAK:
fewer than PC001+PC003 supported.

## Anti-fitting rule

Do not count:
- mere repetition of a formula;
- poetic meter by itself;
- generic prayer performance;
- modern reconstruction choices;
unless the ancient instruction explicitly marks execution as ritually relevant.

## Model upgrade condition

If STRONG_SUPPORT:
upgrade v11 from
"performance may matter"
to
"execution parameters can form part of sacred-token specification in late-antique ritual systems."

Do NOT infer that every Marsanes performance term encodes a unique deity or cosmic level.
