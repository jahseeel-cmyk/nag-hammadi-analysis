## PC001 — procedural performance binding

SUPPORTED.

Three distinct PGM procedures independently bind sacred utterance to execution instructions.

### PGM IV.475-829 — so-called Mithras Liturgy
The ritual prescribes:
- drawing breath from the rays three times;
- "Silence" three times with finger on mouth;
- a long hiss followed by a pop;
- hiss twice + pop twice;
- long bellowing with explicit breath/body effort.

These actions are embedded in the ascent sequence.

### PGM V.1-53 — divinatory/oracular procedure
The text gives unusually explicit vocal instructions:
- alpha with open mouth, undulating like a wave;
- omicron briefly, as a breathed threat;
- eta like a baboon;
- epsilon aspirated;
- upsilon like a shepherd, with drawn-out pronunciation;
- IAO directed to earth, air, heaven.

This is direct evidence that lexical/vocal material was not always specified by letters alone.

### PGM VII.756-794 — lunar invocation
The text enumerates sound-types as companions/signs of the divine name:
- silence,
- popping,
- groaning,
- hissing,
- cry of joy,
- moaning,
- barking,
- bellowing,
- neighing,
- musical sound,
- wind-like sounds,
- coercive sound/emanation.

These are not simply alphabetic spellings; non-lexical sound classes participate in the ritual naming system.

## PC002 — execution-sensitive effect

SUPPORTED WITH NUANCE.

Strongest evidence:
PGM IV links different execution stages to different expected ritual transitions.

Examples:
- triple inhalation -> practitioner is lifted/ascends;
- long hiss + pop + voces -> gods cease rushing and look favorably;
- hiss twice + pop twice -> a new visionary stage follows;
- a long bellow with abdominal effort is prescribed "so that" the five senses are set in motion.

Therefore execution parameters are not decorative stage directions.

Nuance:
we do NOT yet have a clean ancient A/B experiment in which the exact same written token is explicitly pronounced two different ways and assigned two different divine identities.

Thus:
EXECUTION-SENSITIVE RITUAL FUNCTION = supported.
EXECUTION ALONE REDEFINES TOKEN IDENTITY = not yet established.

## PC003 — Iamblichean non-interchangeability

STRONGLY SUPPORTED.

In De Mysteriis VII, Iamblichus argues:
- sacred/divine names are not merely conventional labels;
- translating them does not preserve the same power;
- traditional sacred languages are preferred;
- inherited prayers/names should be preserved without arbitrary alteration.

This is strong theoretical evidence that sacred utterance was understood as form-sensitive and not semantically interchangeable.

## PC004 — ordinary prosodic control

SUPPORTED.

Dionysius of Halicarnassus and the grammatical tradition discuss:
- pitch accent;
- high/low tone;
- quantity;
- rhythm;
- altered musical delivery.

These features are ordinary linguistic/musical properties and do not by themselves imply divine efficacy.

Therefore:
pitch/rhythm/duration are not intrinsically occult.

The ritual distinction lies in their BINDING to a sacred operation.

## PC005 — anti-anachronism

SUPPORTED.

No ancient source in this test supplies:
- exact Hz values;
- 432 Hz;
- planetary frequency assignments;
- modern note labels;
- BPM values.

All such mappings remain excluded.

EXECUTION PARAMETERS CAN FORM PART OF SACRED-TOKEN SPECIFICATION.
