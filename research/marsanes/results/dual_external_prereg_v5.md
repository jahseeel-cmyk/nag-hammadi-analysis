# Dual External Replication v5 — SEALED PREREGISTRATION

Freeze date: 2026-09-19

Targets:
1. Pistis Sophia
2. Books of Jeu

Model under test:
CORRESPONDENTIAL_STATE_SPACE_MODEL_V5

No v5 operator changes are permitted before both texts are scored.

## Why both

Bruce Codex Untitled Text already produced one external prospective replication.
A stronger test is to hold v5 fixed across two additional external corpora rather than revising after the first success.

## Shared predictions for each corpus

DX01 SOURCE->CARRIER->EFFECT
At least one explicit chain:
SOURCE / INTERIOR STATE
-> CARRIER (voice, word, name, image, sign, seal, garment, formula, command, sound)
-> EFFECT (knowledge, revelation, manifestation, salvation, transformation, ascent/return).

DX02 REPRESENTATION
At least one lower form is explicitly an image, likeness, type, seal, garment, sign, name, figure, or indwelling expression of a higher state.

DX03 OPERATIONAL SACRED LANGUAGE
At least one word/name/formula/sound/command is said to do something: reveal, create, open, seal, empower, save, transform, or permit passage.

DX04 BOUNDARY
At least one silence/veil/seal/garment/gate/threshold functions as a transition mechanism rather than only static imagery.

DX05 RETURN / HOMOLOGOUS STATE
At least one return/ascent path reconnects with a state, realm, or source explicitly linked to origin/manifestation.

DX06 ANTI-NUMEROLOGY
At least one qualifying chain and representation relation remain after explicit number words and numerical hierarchies are ignored.

## Decision per corpus

STRONG_REPLICATION:
5-6 of DX01-DX06 supported, with DX01 and DX03 required.

PARTIAL_REPLICATION:
3-4 supported, with at least one of DX01/DX03.

WEAK_OR_NO_REPLICATION:
0-2 supported, or neither DX01 nor DX03 supported.

## Combined decision

DUAL_STRONG:
both corpora STRONG_REPLICATION.

MIXED_EXTERNAL:
one STRONG and one PARTIAL/WEAK.

NO_DUAL_REPLICATION:
neither STRONG.
