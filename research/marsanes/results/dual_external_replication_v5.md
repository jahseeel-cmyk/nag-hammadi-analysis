## Books of Jeu

### DX01 — SOURCE -> CARRIER -> EFFECT
STRONGLY SUPPORTED.

Primary-text level evidence includes:
- Jeu gives voice;
- the voice comes forth as an emanation;
- the first voice is explicitly said to come forth from Jeu.

This is a direct SOURCE -> VOICE -> EMANATION chain.

A second operational pattern:
seal + seal-name + spoken defence
-> guardians withdraw
-> initiate proceeds upward.

### DX02 — explicit representation relation
SUPPORTED.

The books pair:
- graphic seal,
- seal name,
- cipher,
- realm/treasury,
- divine/guardian identity.

The seal functions as a represented identity/authorization key for a specific treasury or aeon.

### DX03 — operational sacred language
STRONGLY SUPPORTED.

The text repeatedly instructs the ascender to:
- seal oneself;
- say the seal's name;
- recite defensive names/formulae.

The guardians then withdraw and passage becomes possible.

### DX04 — operational boundary
STRONGLY SUPPORTED.

Gates, treasuries, aeons, watchers, and veils are literal ritual-transition boundaries.
Names/seals cause watchers and veils to withdraw/open.

### DX05 — return / homologous state
NOT ESTABLISHED in the reviewed evidence.

Ascent from the body through aeons to the Treasury of Light is explicit.
However, the reviewed material did not securely establish that the destination is explicitly the same state/place from which the soul originally emanated.

This prediction is therefore scored FALSE rather than being inferred.

### DX06 — anti-numerology
SUPPORTED.

Even if ciphers and numbered aeons are removed, the core mechanism remains:
seal + name + formula -> boundary withdrawal -> upward passage.

Decision:
STRONG_REPLICATION (5/6).
