# Correspondential State-Space Model v5

Freeze date: 2026-09-19

## Why v5 exists

The tractate findings directory and SYNTHESIS_the_whole_picture.md add a large amount of cross-text internal evidence. Because the synthesis explicitly surveys all 48 tractates, the Nag Hammadi Library can no longer supply pristine internal holdouts. From this point forward:

- NHL texts = training / retrospective cross-text evidence;
- external corpora = prospective holdouts.

This is a methodological upgrade, not a setback.

## Core claim

The recurring architecture is better modeled as SAME STATE, VARIABLE FORM across degrees than as a fixed cipher.

Let S be an underlying state/principle and R_d(S) its representation at degree d.

Downward transduction:
R_high(S) -> R_mid(S) -> R_low(S)

Upward/return transduction:
R_low(S) -> recognition/participation -> R_mid(S) -> source

The representation can change while a functional or relational invariant is retained.

## Frozen operators

C1 PROJECT(A,B)
A higher state is expressed as an image/type/likeness/shadow/form B.

C2 ARTICULATE(A,B)
An interior state becomes voice/word/name/sound/form B.

C3 INHABIT(A,B)
A higher state/principle is explicitly present in, worn by, or operating through a lower vessel/form B.

C4 DIFFERENTIATE(A,{B...})
One source/state differentiates into multiple forms, powers, organs, names, or degrees.

C5 RECURSE(A,B)
A relation repeats at successive levels: image of image, shadow of shadow, angels of angels, etc.

C6 MISATTRIBUTE(A,B)
A lower receiver claims received power/light as its own or mistakes the carrier for the source.

C7 RECOGNIZE(A,B)
Perception/knowledge identifies the higher state through its lower representation.

C8 PARTICIPATE(A,B)
Seeing/knowing/receiving produces participation or transformation into B.

C9 BOUNDARY(A,B)
Silence, veil, seal, garment, or threshold marks/mediates transition between states.

C10 ASCEND(A,B)
Subject moves/returns toward a higher homologous state.

C11 DESCEND(A,B)
Higher state manifests/enters/proceeds toward a lower expression.

C12 REVERSE_PATH(P)
The same or homologous path is traversed in the opposite direction.

## Anti-overreach rules

- A symbolic resemblance is not an operator.
- A numerical recurrence is not a state identity.
- Swedenborg, Hermetic, Platonic, Kabbalistic, or other later systems remain comparators unless independently predictive.
- Name etymologies require philological verification.
- Exact body/angel correspondences from AJ must be separated into manuscript observation, etymology, functional anatomy, and esoteric reading.
- No claim that the 48 tractates are literally one ancient authored composition is implied by this model.
- Cross-text recurrence can reflect shared milieu, dependence, redaction, or common source as well as a deeper formal system.

## Falsification

Downgrade v5 if external holdouts show:
- no explicit source->carrier->formed/effect chains;
- no independently identifiable homologous states;
- operator coverage requires flexible relabeling;
- generic religious language performs equally well;
- apparent support depends on number symbolism or post-hoc etymology.
