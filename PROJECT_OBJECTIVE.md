# Hermes / Marsanes Research Charter

This file is the canonical objective for the project and should be consulted before extending the research.

## Mission
Reconstruct and test lost sacred-language technologies while treating historical-philological, formal/acoustic, experiential, and mystical interpretations as distinct but potentially simultaneous evidence layers. Established scholarship is a control and source base, not a ceiling on discovery.

## Primary goals
1. Recover lost/damaged structures, words, names, chants, ritual sequences, and operator rules where evidence permits.
2. Find genuinely new correspondences and implications, then preregister and test them.
3. Build predictive models capable of restoring withheld/damaged material or predicting independent parallels.
4. Make surviving chants, sacred names, angelic nomenclature, symbols, and ritual mechanics understandable through transliteration, pronunciation, literal sense, function, and evidence status.
5. Create reproducible experimental reconstructions when complete ancient procedures are lost.
6. Test mystical efficacy as an open hypothesis without either assuming or dismissing it.

## Mandatory evidence labels
- ATTESTED — directly present in primary evidence.
- RECONSTRUCTED — philological/structural restoration or inference.
- EXPERIMENTAL — our reproducible implementation.
- INTUITIVE/CORRESPONDENTIAL — novel hypothesis logged before testing.

## Evaluation
A model gains weight through textual fit, structural compression, new predictions, successful restoration, independent cross-corpus prediction, formal/acoustic reproducibility, phenomenological reproducibility, and survival under adversarial controls.

This is a research hypothesis, not an established historical fact.

## Required outputs
- Ritual Operator Lexicon.
- Source-by-source ritual dossiers.
- Machine-readable operator matrix.
- Pronunciation alternatives with uncertainty.
- Reconstruction ledger.
- Prediction/falsification ledger.
- Experimental protocol and observation log.
- Updated XMind after each major result.

## Guardrails
Technical and mystical are not opposites. Structural analogy is not genealogy. A novel correspondence is neither dismissed nor accepted automatically: it is converted into a prediction wherever possible. Never present a modern reconstruction as a surviving ancient ritual.
