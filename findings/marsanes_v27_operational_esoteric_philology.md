# v27 Methodological Reset — Operative Esoteric Philology

## Principle
Technical and mystical are not mutually exclusive explanatory categories. Marsanes can be read simultaneously as a work using Greek grammatical/phonological knowledge and as a text that treats sounds, names, silence, and angelic/cosmic structures as ritually or metaphysically operative.

## Four evidence states
1. ATTESTED — directly present in primary text/manuscript.
2. RECONSTRUCTED — restored or inferred from damaged/incomplete evidence.
3. EXPERIMENTAL — our test implementation of a proposed operator.
4. INTUITIVE/CORRESPONDENTIAL — novel cross-system hypothesis, preregistered before testing.

## Evaluation criteria for the new research program
- textual fit;
- structural compression;
- number of genuinely new implications;
- successful restoration/prediction of missing structure;
- cross-corpus prediction without adding ad hoc rules;
- phenomenological reproducibility;
- acoustic/formal reproducibility;
- adversarial-control survival;
- distinction between historical genealogy and structural analogy.

## Experimental Marsanes Rite v0.1
This is a modern research reconstruction, not a recovered complete ancient ritual.

1. SILENCE — 30–60 seconds.
2. SEVEN VOWELS — A E Ē I O U Ō, one sustained breath each.
3. TRIPLICATION — AAA / EEE / ĒĒĒ / III / OOO / UUU / ŌŌŌ.
4. SYLLABIC LADDER — BAGADAZATHA; BEGEDEZETHE; BĒGĒDĒZĒTHĒ; BIGIDIZITHI; BOGODOZOTHO; BUGUDUZUTHU; BŌGŌDŌZŌTHŌ.
5. PERFORMANCE GATE — vary only one factor per trial: beat, pitch, silence interval, or impulse/onset.
6. UNIFICATION — finish on a single sustained vowel or hum and record whether the sequence perceptually converges toward one sound/accord.
7. LOG — imagery, felt sense, semantic impressions, timing, audio, and acoustic measures.

## Enochian comparator
Sloane MS 3189 / Liber Loagaeth is securely catalogued as a 1583 Dee/Kelley manuscript whose later folios contain 49x49 letter-number grids. Use it as a late operational comparator, not a historical source for Marsanes.

First Call incipit: OL SONF VORSG.
Approximate historical sense: "I reign over you..." (full translation is substantially longer).

Names appearing in Dee's ritual apparatus include Raphael, Gabriel, Uriel; and the five names Corabiel, Zedekieil, Madimiel, Semeliel, Nogahel.
