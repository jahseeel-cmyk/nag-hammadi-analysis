# Marsanes v26 — Greek Performance-Science Control

## Question
Could Marsanes' execution layer derive substantially from ordinary Greek rhetoric/music/phonetics rather than from a uniquely esoteric performance technology?

## Control result
Dionysius of Halicarnassus, *On Literary Composition*, explicitly places phonological classification, pronunciation, breath, quantity, pitch, rhythm, and delivery inside one technical account:
- vowels / semivowels / mutes;
- long, short, and common vowels;
- breath and articulation;
- pitch movement in spoken language;
- melody of speech;
- rhythm and timing;
- duration differences produced by prefixes/consonant clusters.

Quintilian likewise describes voice as governed by rhythm and melody.

Therefore Marsanes cannot claim novelty merely for:
- connecting phonological classes with performance;
- pitch;
- rhythm/beat;
- duration/breath;
- controlled pronunciation.

## Combined-control decomposition
When Greek performance theory is combined with Marcus and ritual controls, most ingredients of the Marsanes mechanism are independently covered.

Heuristic Wolfram set-cover coding:
- 9 of 11 coded features are covered by controls;
- residual features are:
  1. active semivowel recruitment;
  2. horme/impulse as part of the local execution bundle.

This is a diagnostic model, not a historical probability.

## Major correction
The distinctive feature is no longer best described as an "execution-control layer" in general.

A better description is:

**CROSS-CLASS SACRALIZED PHONOLOGICAL OPERATOR**

Marsanes appears to take ordinary Greek performance phonetics and an older Gnostic sacred-letter cosmology and bind them into a local rule where execution variables cause one phonological class to recruit another.
